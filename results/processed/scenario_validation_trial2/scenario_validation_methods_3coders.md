# Three-Coder Scenario Foundation Validation (Trial 2)

Three independent human coders labelled all 50 English scenarios while blinded to the intended foundation labels, model outputs, and one another's labels. For each scenario, coders selected one primary Moral Foundations Theory category from Care/Harm, Fairness/Cheating, Loyalty/Betrayal, Authority/Subversion, and Sanctity/Degradation. Pairwise raw agreement was Coder A vs Coder B 74.00%, Coder A vs Coder C 68.00%, Coder B vs Coder C 70.00%. Pairwise Cohen's kappa was Coder A vs Coder B 0.671551, Coder A vs Coder C 0.597586, Coder B vs Coder C 0.625375, with a mean pairwise kappa of 0.631504; Fleiss' kappa across all three coders was 0.629297. Majority-vote labels matched the intended foundation for 78.00% of scenarios overall. Foundation-specific majority agreement is reported in `scenario_validation_by_foundation_3coders.csv`; for Authority/Subversion scenarios specifically, majority-vote agreement with the intended foundation was 50.00% (n = 10).
