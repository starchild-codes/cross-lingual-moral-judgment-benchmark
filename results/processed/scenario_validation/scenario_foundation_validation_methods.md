# Scenario Foundation Validation Methods

Two independent human coders labelled all 50 English scenarios in a blinded scenario-validation interface. Coders were shown only the scenario ID, English scenario text, five Moral Foundations Theory response options, and an optional notes box; they were not shown intended foundation labels, Coder A labels, model outputs, language condition, or translation/adaptation status. Coders selected the primary MFT category from: Care/Harm, Fairness/Cheating, Loyalty/Betrayal, Authority/Subversion, and Sanctity/Degradation.

Raw Coder A vs Coder B agreement was 76.00% (Cohen's kappa = 0.697428). Coder A matched the intended scenario foundation for 72.00% of scenarios, and Coder B matched the intended foundation for 82.00%; the average coder-intended agreement was 77.00%. Both coders matched the intended foundation for 66.00% of scenarios, while at least one coder matched the intended foundation for 88.00%. For intended Authority/Subversion scenarios specifically (n = 10), Coder A agreement was 30.00%, Coder B agreement was 70.00%, average coder-intended agreement was 50.00%, both-coder match rate was 30.00%, and at-least-one-coder match rate was 70.00%. 12 coder disagreements require adjudication before an adjudicated scenario-label validation statistic can be reported.
